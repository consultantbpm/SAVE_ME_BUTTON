Save Me Button turns your Wear OS watch into a personal emergency button. When you need help, just hold the watch's physical button to start an emergency sequence - right from your wrist.

HOW IT WORKS
Hold the button and Save Me Button will:
- Send an SMS with your location - GPS coordinates plus a map link - to your emergency contacts
- Call up to 3 contacts, one after another
- Keep repeating until someone answers

If your watch has its own cellular connection, everything runs directly from the watch. Otherwise it works through the companion phone app. Either way, help is one button-hold away.

QUICK CANCEL
Triggered it by accident? Cancel the whole sequence with just a few taps.

THE ALARM IS ALWAYS FREE
The SOS emergency function is free forever and is never locked behind a paywall. Sending the SMS, calling your contacts and the auto-repeat escalation will always work at no cost. Your safety is not a premium feature.

OPTIONAL UNLOCK
A single one-time purchase of EUR 2.99 unlocks just one convenience: editing the custom text of your emergency SMS message. Everything safety-related stays free - this unlock is purely optional.

PHONE COMPANION
Set up your contacts and message on the companion phone app, then let the watch do the rest.

WHY SAVE ME BUTTON
It is a watch-button SOS that automatically escalates from SMS to calls and keeps trying until someone answers - and the alarm always stays free.

Add Save Me Button and carry a safety net on your wrist.
